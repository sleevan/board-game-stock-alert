Blood Rage Valhalla - Board Game

Product Info

Description

Number of Players: 3-5
Playing Time: 60 Minutes
Recommended Ages: 14+


Rage into the afterlife in Blood Rage: Valhalla! Take up arms and empower your clan beyond their mortal limits, competing against your rivals for glory, honor, and the favor of the Gods.


  • Fight fiercely against the other clans and embark on legendary quests to win your gods favor!
  • Highly detailed miniatures are visually stunning and offer an impressive table presence.
  • Play cards to add abilities to your clan and leaders and summon mighty monsters. Tailor your unique strengths and strategies as you play.
